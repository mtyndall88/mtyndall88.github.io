About Me

I’m a Marine Corps veteran and software developer with a focus on building reliable, trustworthy software. During my nine years of military service, I developed experience in high-pressure environments that demanded precision, critical thinking, and teamwork.

I built it end to end with Node.js, Express, and MySQL, and I develop with agentic AI workflows as a core part of my process — more than 400 of the roughly 590 commits were built with Claude Code. I also used the Anthropic Claude API to label and independently audit a 1,500-note dataset that measures and tunes the platform’s rule-based risk engine.

Now based in Southwest Florida, I’m a husband and father of two, focused on continuing to grow as a developer. I’m seeking a full-time remote software developer role where I can apply my technical skills, contribute to mission-driven teams, and maintain balance with what matters most at home.
